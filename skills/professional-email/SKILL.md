# Professional Email and Messages

## When to use this skill
- Sending an important email to a client, manager, or teacher
- Following up on an unpaid invoice or a silent client
- Saying no, asking for more time, or delivering bad news professionally

## Workflow
1. Ask for or identify: who receives it, what outcome the user wants, and the relationship (new client, regular client, boss, teacher). Tone depends on all three.
2. Read the rough draft for the real message underneath. Keep every fact (dates, amounts, deliverables) exactly as given - polish the language, never change the facts.
3. Choose the tone level and state it: Polite (first follow-up), Clear (second, with dates), Firm (third or later, with consequences stated calmly). For payment follow-ups, count which attempt this is.
4. Structure: subject line that states the topic, one-line context, the ask in its own line, a specific date or next step, professional close. Short paragraphs, no wall of text.
5. Deliver the clean version first. Under it, give 1-2 alternate subject lines and, if the draft was emotional, one line explaining what was softened and why.
6. If the input is in another language or mixed language, translate the meaning faithfully - do not add promises, apologies, or commitments the user did not make.

## Rules - never break these
- Never add a commitment, discount, deadline, or apology the user did not write or approve.
- Never send or claim to send the email - output is a draft for the user to send.
- Firm does not mean threatening. State facts and next steps; leave emotion in the deleted draft.
- One email, one main ask. If the user has two topics, recommend two emails.

## Output format
- Subject: the chosen subject line
- Email body - ready to copy, in the chosen tone
- Tone used - Polite / Clear / Firm + why
- Alternatives - 1-2 other subject lines

## Example
**User:** Make this professional, it is my second follow-up: bhai payment ka kya hua, 2 week ho gaye hain, kaam to kab ka deliver kar diya tha, please bata dein

**Good output (excerpt):** Subject: Payment follow-up - Invoice 12, delivered 1 Oct. Body (Clear tone): I hope you are well. I am following up on Invoice 12 for the delivered website work, due on 1 Oct. Could you please confirm the payment date this week? If any document is needed from my side, I will send it today. Facts kept, frustration removed, specific ask added.
